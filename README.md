This is a repository for the genome wide association study (GWAS) protocol I made during my MSc program at McMaster University, in Dr. Jianping Xu's lab *([University Profile](https://experts.mcmaster.ca/display/jpxu), [Lab Website](https://xulabmcmaster.wordpress.com/pi-biography/))*.  

This github repository was written to submit to the BIO722 (Introduction to Bioinformatic Methods) graduate course at McMaster University and thus only uses a subset of samples.  

This protocol was used as the foundation for three of my published papers <sup>[1](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC7713013/), [2](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC8227032/), [3](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC8538161/)</sup> and for a poster presented at the 31st Fungal Genetics Conference based on my thesis. The poster, titled ["Genomic and Genetic Analyses of Antifungal Drug Resistance in *Aspergillus fumigatus*"](https://genetics-gsa.org/fungal-2022/wp-content/uploads/sites/36/2022/03/220316-Fungal22-Program-Book-v3.pdf), was presented by my supervisor Dr. Jianping Xu and I was responsible for drafting the poster contents and layout.
