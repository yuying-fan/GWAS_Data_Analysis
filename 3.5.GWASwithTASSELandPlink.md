We will first analyse for cryptic relatedness

Looking at ```plink.imiss```, we will be only keep one sample from each group as the representative of the group. By looking at the "F_MISS" column, which column represents the proportion of missing SNPs in the individual, we will be using this information to keep the strain with the highest call rate.

For each group, DRR022916, DRR022925, and SRR8759706 had the lowest "F_MISS" value and thus will be kept as the individual group representative. Therefore, the remaining 7 strains will be removed from further analysis.

```
nano remove_related_strains.txt
```
